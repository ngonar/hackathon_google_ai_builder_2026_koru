About Eleanor

35 Years Dedication

For over thirty-five years, Eleanor shaped minds and spirits as Head of Literature, unveiling the cadence of poetry to hundreds of young scholars who still speak of her gentle guidance. Beyond the lecture hall, her heart belonged to the damp soil of Christchurch.

St. Matthew's, 1988

The Rose Sanctuary

Her botanical sanctuary was a haven for wild bees, rare native ferns, and any wandering neighbor seeking a warm cup of Earl Grey. Above all, her true joy blossomed in her three grandchildren—Maya, Noah, and little Rose—to whom she gifted handmade fairy tales and dried hydrangea pressed between parchment.

Life Journey Milestones

1952 March 14

Born in Dunedin, New Zealand

Born to Arthur and Margaret Sterling, amidst coastal winds and hillsides of yellow gorse. A precocious reader from her earliest days.

1974 Otago Honors

Graduated with Honors in English Literature

University of Otago. Authored an award-winning dissertation on Romantic poets and natural landscape imagery in early colonial poetry.

1978 November 18

Married Thomas Vance

Together they hand-crafted their family sanctuary home in Cashmere Hills, planting over 200 native fern specimens and silver beeches.

1985 – 2010 25 Year Chair

Head of Literature at St. Matthew’s

Mentored generations of writers, initiated the beloved annual Spring Shakespeare Garden Festival, and fostered an enduring love for words.

2015 Autumn Equinox

Published “Botanical Memories”

Her collection of intimate sonnets weaving New Zealand plant ecology with reflections on maternal tenderness, privately pressed and shared.
