Life & Remembrance

Botanist & Matriarch

Peggy possessed an enduring grace and an observant soul that could spot the earliest alpine buttercup blooming through melting snow. For over eight decades, she cultivated wonder—first across the dramatic ridges of Otago, then within her 50-year loving partnership with Arthur, and ultimately in the gentle sanctuary of her own garden where she brewed quiet morning teas and shared boundless affection with her six cherished grandchildren.

Life Chapters

5 Milestones
1942

Born in Dunedin

Welcomed into the world on an early winter morning near the Otago Peninsula, discovering her love for coastal plants.

1964

Master of Botany at Otago

Graduated with high honors, cataloging over 300 endemic tussock and alpine species across the Southern Alps.

1971

Married Arthur Sutherland

United in marriage under blooming kōwhai trees; together founded the Highlands Botanical Reserve to conserve indigenous ecosystems.

1988

Published Fieldwork Monograph

Authored ‘Wildflowers of the Southern Alps’, an illustrated guidebook celebrated throughout natural science institutions.

2012

Community Sanctuary Dedication

The Sutherland Community Garden was permanently dedicated in her honor as an educational sanctuary for youth and families.
